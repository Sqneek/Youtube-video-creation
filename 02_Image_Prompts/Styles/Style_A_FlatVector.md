# Style A — Flat Vector

**Flow project:** Footnotes Image Creation

## Art style / medium
Flat 2D vector illustration, cel-shaded, clean black ink outlines.

## Character
- Head: plain circle, flat off-white matching the body's skin tone, one continuous color. Cel-shaded blocking for form only — never realistic shading.
- Face: simple dot eyes, thin line mouth. No nose, no other detail.
- One fixed stickman design for every human figure — no per-character redesign.
- Whole figure, not just the head: same flat color fills and clean even-weight ink outlines throughout — arms, legs, hands, clothing. Never realistic anatomy, skin texture, wrinkles, or muscle definition.
- Named/bearded characters (e.g. Vespasian): beard is one flat shape in outline, not individual hairs. Face stays as simple/blank as every other character — a beard or laurel wreath is a role detail, not license for realistic rendering.

## Background / scene
Detailed, textured, weathered — rust, grime, water stains, cracked stone, fabric folds.

## Lighting
Strong single-source directional light — diagonal light shafts through haze/dust/smoke, visible particles in the beam, high contrast between lit and shadow. Overcast/foggy scenes: flat, even, cool light instead.

## Color palette
Desaturated, muted tones throughout. Warm khaki/sepia/dust for sunlit daylight/ruin scenes, cool grey-blue for overcast/sea/fog scenes, amber/orange for fire/torchlight or emphasis, deep blue/near-monochrome for night scenes, warm lamplight gold for interiors, pink-gold for dawn/dusk, slightly more saturated warm tone for modern-era scenes.

## Camera angles — exactly 3, pick one per prompt
- **Wide shot** — establishing/scale; camera distant, subject small in frame; use for scope, geography, or a crowd/group.
- **Medium shot** — eye-level, one or a few figures, mid-action; the default for most narrative beats.
- **Close-up** — single subject or detail, tight framing; use for emotional emphasis or a specific small detail (an object, a hand, a face).

**Do not replicate** (edit-layer artifacts from reference footage — never in a Flow prompt): burned-in captions, title text overlay, video player UI, reactive post-effects over a character.

## A-roll — Narrator
- **Tag:** `@Narrator` — pre-built template in Google Flow, no design description needed.
- **Use for:** host/cutaway moments, not script-illustration scenes.
- **Prompt template:** `@Narrator, [action/pose description].` — no background, lighting, or camera field needed unless deliberately placing him in a specific setting.
- **Sequence rule:** one image per narrator appearance — no burst/animation sequence.
- **Placement:** the Narrator appears 2–3 times total across the video, never in the hook. Use these at moments where something is being explained directly to the viewer, or where the beat doesn't need a B-roll visual to land. Distribute placements across the body/close rather than clustering them.
